We propose an environmental sound synthesis from visual onomatopoeia and sound-source images  {% cite visual_onoma_to_wave %}.

External project page is [Here](https://sarulab-speech.github.io/demo_visual-onoma-to-wave/).

At that time, the DALL-E prototype had just been released, and I got the impression that it was difficult to effectively use methods like automated image generation. 
However, I believe it's now possible to create a much higher-quality method.
